AARVA — The Aroma of South | Website concept
================================================

Files
- index.html: Main responsive website
- styles.css: Layout, colours, typography and responsive styling
- script.js: Interactive category-based menu and mobile navigation

How to preview
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. An internet connection is required for Google Fonts and remote food photography.

Before presenting/publishing
- This is an independent website concept/redesign proposal, not an authorised AARVA website.
- The business already appears to have a website at https://aarva.co/. Present this as a redesign concept.
- Confirm permission from the business owner before publishing, using their logo/brand assets, or representing this as the official site.
- Verify all menu prices, dishes, address, phone number and opening hours with the owner. Prices are indicative and may change.
- Replace stock photography with owner-approved restaurant and food photographs for the final production website.
- Confirm WhatsApp ordering is enabled on the supplied business number.
- The website is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript; it does not include an ordering backend, payment gateway, admin panel or hosting.
